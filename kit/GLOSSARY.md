# Glossary

The app's own words, each with one meaning. Written by the clarifier the moment the test
author explains a term (maintaining-glossary skill). No implementation details.

The entries below are examples for the Expense Tracker demo app. Replace them with your app's terms.

**Expense**: One cost an employee records, with a description and an amount in EUR.

**Total**: The sum of all expenses in the list, shown as "Total: €0.00" under the list.

**Status message**: The one-line message under the form that confirms an action ("Added Taxi") or explains an error.
_Avoid_: notification, toast, alert.

**Group**: A set of test cases signed off together, e.g. Expenses, Login. Comes from the Group column of the Excel.

**Waiting room**: The list of patients who have checked in for a visit, shown on the Today board as "Waiting: <name>". It is empty when nobody has checked in.
_Avoid_: lobby.
