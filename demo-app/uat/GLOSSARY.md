# Lumen Clinic

Words the desk uses. One meaning each.

- MRN: the medical record number. Unique for each patient. Ada Lovelace is 1001. Alan Turing is 1002.
- On hand: how many units of a stock item are in the cupboard.
- Low: an item with fewer than 5 on hand.
- Follow-up: the next planned visit. "not set" means nobody has planned one.
- Clinic day: 11 October 2026. The desk does not use today's real date.
- Unpaid: an invoice that has not been paid. INV-100 is €40.00 and starts unpaid. INV-101 is €15.00 and starts paid. The next invoice raised is INV-102.
- Allergy: a medicine the patient must not be given. Ada Lovelace is allergic to Penicillin. Alan Turing has none, shown as "Allergy: none".
- Booked: a visit that has not been checked in. Arrived means the patient is in the waiting room.
- Waiting: the board line for patients who are Arrived. It is absent until someone is checked in.
- Pending: a lab order with no result yet. Full blood count for Ada Lovelace starts pending.
- Referral: a request a manager sends to a specialty. An employee cannot send one.
